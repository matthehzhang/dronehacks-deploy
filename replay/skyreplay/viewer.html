About this replay

There is no ground truth in a bag. Each trace is an onboard estimate, placed on the map through the declared launch pose. Disagreement between traces, a path through a wall, or live-map walls that do not line up with the course map are the signs the drone was not where it thought it was. The course map is an approximate reconstruction, not a survey (see map README).
